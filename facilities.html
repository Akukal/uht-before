Control & process flow

Centralized control.
Connected stages.

Equipment operation and material transfer are managed centrally through a Programmable Logic Controller (PLC) panel.

Students can observe automation, process sequencing, and troubleshooting.

This page describes observed facility features. Detailed machine specifications, throughput, operating parameters, and availability should be requested from the facility manager.
UHT specification

Published
parameters.

Figures below come from published POLIJE documentation. Confirm operational parameters before use.

Capacity (published) Up to 1,000 litres per process
Process temperature 135–150 °C
Duration 2–5 seconds
Components Heat Exchanger · Holding Tube · Filling Machine
Packaging Sterile packaging system
Capacity of 1,000 litres is a published specification, not a guarantee of external service capacity. Current operational readiness must be confirmed by the manager.
