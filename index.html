A Teaching Factory with three roles

Learning meets
real-world industry.

TEFA UHT connects practical learning, campus production, and industrial research in one pilot-scale environment.

01

Learning & Practicum

Students learn production workflows, equipment operation, and troubleshooting across dairy and liquid food processing.

02

Campus Production

Pasteurized milk and selected liquid food products support canteens, orientation, graduation, and campus events.

03

Industrial R&D

A pilot-scale setting for product trials with industry partners — subject to project fit and facility readiness.

Industrial product exploration

Test the process
before the next step.

Explore liquid food processing ideas in a pilot-scale environment, at a scale that makes early project discussion meaningful.

Production runs may require roughly 300 to 2,000 litres of raw material. Project fit, material volume, process route, packaging, and schedule are reviewed together with the TEFA UHT team.
