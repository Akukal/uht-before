Educational summary based on the TEFA UHT interview document and published POLIJE references. It is not a substitute for validated process specifications, food-safety procedures, or the storage instructions printed on a product label.
01 / PASTEURIZATION

Short-time heating

Heating at 70–80 °C for 15–20 seconds, intended to reduce pathogenic microorganisms. Refrigerated storage remains necessary.

02 / STERILIZATION

Higher heat exposure

Heating at 121 °C for 5 minutes. A stronger heat treatment, with possible changes in nutritional value and colour.

03 / UHT

Very high temperature, short time

135–150 °C for 2–5 seconds, followed by sterile packaging for extended unopened storage.

Storage matters

Processing is only
part of the story.

Storage conditions and packaging play an important role after processing.

Pasteurized milk requires refrigeration. UHT products rely on an appropriate sterile packaging system for unopened shelf stability. Once opened, follow the product label and keep the product refrigerated as instructed.

For sterilized products, storage guidance should always follow the validated product and packaging specifications.
