Visit requirements

How a visit moves.

Visits are arranged through a request and confirmation flow, not a public ticketing system.

01

Submit

Send your institution, group size, purpose, and a proposed date.

02

Review

The manager reviews availability and scope.

03

Confirm

The team confirms the schedule by reply.

04

Visit

Visit takes place per the confirmed arrangements.

A visit is only scheduled after the request has been reviewed and confirmed by the TEFA UHT manager.
