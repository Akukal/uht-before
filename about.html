About TEFA UHT

A learning environment shaped by industry.

TEFA UHT connects hands-on learning, liquid food processing, campus production, and product development.

Our background

From TEFA Milk
to TEFA UHT.

Established in 2023, the facility was initially known as TEFA Milk before adopting the TEFA UHT name.

Its scope expanded from milk to a wider range of liquid food products. Today it operates as a mini-industry environment at Politeknik Negeri Jember — bringing production practice and industry-oriented learning into one setting.

Note: the naming chronology and establishment year are based on facility confirmation, not yet an independent historical audit.
The three pillars

Three functions.
One ecosystem.

Three connected roles shape the TEFA UHT ecosystem.

01

Learning & Practicum

Students from animal science, food technology, and engineering learn industrial processing stages, equipment operation, and troubleshooting — including machine and PLC systems.

02

Campus Production

Liquid food products support internal campus needs — canteen supply and events such as student orientation, graduation ceremonies, and campus anniversaries.

03

Industrial R&D

A place for pilot-scale product trials and industry collaboration. Every project requires an initial discussion to assess material volume, process needs, and equipment readiness.
